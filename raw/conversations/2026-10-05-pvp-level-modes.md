# 2026-10-05 — PvP level modes; battle rules and grid numbers approved

Kind: design conversation. The designer asked why every Peerling fights at level
50 in PvP. The LLM explained (levels can't be verified; fairness) and offered
options: A keep level 50, B real levels verified by replaying all battles, C the
players choose per battle between "Fair (level 50)" and unverified "Real
levels", D level brackets. It recommended C.

## Designer's answer (verbatim)

> Go with C and the rest of battle ruels are approved, also grid number are approved

## Summary of what was decided

- PvP level modes (option C): the challenger offers "Fair" (everyone at level
  50, the default) or "Real levels" (actual levels, not verified); both players
  must agree.
- The proposed battle rules are approved (Q-039).
- The proposed grid and encounter numbers are approved (Q-040).
