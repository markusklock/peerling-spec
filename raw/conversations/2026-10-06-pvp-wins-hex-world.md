# 2026-10-06 — PvP win counter, first encounter, hexagon world layout

Kind: design conversation. Follow-up to
[2026-10-06-review-decisions](2026-10-06-review-decisions.md). The LLM had
asked about the guaranteed first encounter (review item 12) and offered three
layouts (A, B, C) for the designer's hexagon world idea.

## Designer's answers (verbatim)

> 1. I made a mistake, I was talking about 7 when I accidently wrote 12. with nothing to lose or gain on PvP it feel meaningless. If we add at least a counter of battles won you at least get something from PvP
> 2. B

## Summary of what was decided

- The earlier comment written under "12" was meant for review item 7 (PvP
  starts at full HP and changes nothing afterwards). So item 12 (the
  guaranteed first encounter, as proposed) was covered by "All others are
  approved", and item 7 also stands.
- The designer wants PvP to give the player something: at least a counter of
  battles won. Accepted: a PvP win counter. The LLM's suggested recording and
  verification design (signed states, `pvp-result` events) remains a proposal.
- World layout option B: a central Plains hexagon where everyone spawns, with
  12 wedge-shaped biome sectors (one per biome, Plains included) running from
  the hexagon to the border. Exact sizes, orientation and biome order are LLM
  proposals.
