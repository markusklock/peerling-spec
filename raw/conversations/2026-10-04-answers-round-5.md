# 2026-10-04 — Answers to open questions (round 5)

Kind: design conversation. The designer answered the four numbered questions
asked after round 4. The questions are paraphrased; the answers are verbatim.

1. Approve the draft type effectiveness chart? (Q-008)
2. Approve the XP curve and wild-level numbers? (Q-030)

4. What next: battle details (switching, items, catch chance, team size),
   world generation (biomes, terrain, landmarks), camera and visual style
   (Q-012), or content moderation (Q-007)?

> 1. approved

## Summary of what was decided

- The type chart is approved (resolves Q-008).
- The XP curve and wild-level numbers are approved (resolves Q-030).
- The encounter-seed design is approved, including drand (resolves Q-029).
- No items in battles in the first version.
- The designer asked for suggestions on catch chance and team size. The
  suggestions were filed as proposals in `wiki/gameplay/catching.md`.
- World generation has 12 biomes, one per type; each biome gives its type an
  increased chance of appearing.
- Camera: top-down view of a 3D world. Visual style: colorful (resolves Q-012).
- No content moderation. The game is a free, self-hosted hobby project with no
  company or brand at risk, and wild or meme Peerlings may even help
  popularity. If it becomes a real problem, the operator will most likely shut
  the game down (resolves Q-007 → D-0010).
