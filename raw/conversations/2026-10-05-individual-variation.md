# 2026-10-05 — Individual variation of Peerlings

Kind: design conversation. The LLM offered four options for variation between
individual Peerlings of the same species and level (Q-037): (a) none, (b)
random stat spread of about ±10% per stat, (c) natures (+10% / −10%), (d) rare
cosmetic color variants (e.g. 1 in 500). It recommended (d), optionally (c),
and asked how rare variants should be.

## Designer's answer (verbatim)

> Go with B and D

## Summary of what was decided

- (b) Every individual Peerling gets a random stat spread of up to ±10% per
  stat.
- (d) Rare cosmetic color variants exist.
- Not answered, so proposed by the LLM: variant rarity (1 in 500), whether the
  stat spread counts in PvP, and whether players can see it.
