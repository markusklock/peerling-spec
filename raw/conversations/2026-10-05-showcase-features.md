# 2026-10-05 — Additional peer-to-peer showcase features

Kind: design conversation. The designer asked for more modern p2p and browser
technology that would add something meaningful while showcasing the tech. The
LLM offered: 1 battle replays as CIDs, 2 spectating PvP battles over pubsub,
3 shareable Peerling cards and player profiles via IPNS and the IPFS Service
Worker Gateway, 4 game rules as content-addressed WebAssembly, 5 scheduled
world events with drand timelock encryption, 6 linking a device by code/QR
over a direct WebRTC connection, 7 a live world feed over pubsub; plus minor
ideas (CAR files, verifiable-credential achievements, WebGPU compute effects).

## Designer's answer (verbatim)

> 2,3,6 and 7

## Summary of what was decided

- Adopted: spectating PvP battles (2), shareable Peerling cards and player
  profiles (3), linking a device (6), live world feed (7) (→ D-0016).
- Not adopted: battle replays, WebAssembly rules on IPFS, timelock events, and
  the minor ideas.
