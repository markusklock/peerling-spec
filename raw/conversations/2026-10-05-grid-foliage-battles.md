# 2026-10-05 — Grid movement, foliage encounters, battle design request

Kind: design conversation.

## Designer's statement (verbatim)

> Perhaps we should make the game a bit like pokemon on gameboy, that the player walks on an invisible grid, from tile to tile?
> Yeah wild peerlings should be in tall grass(or similar foliage depending on the biome)
>
> How do you suggest we design the battles?

## Summary of what was decided

- Movement is tile-based, like Pokémon on the Game Boy: the player walks on an
  invisible grid, from tile to tile.
- Wild Peerlings are encountered in tall grass, or similar foliage depending on
  the biome.
- The designer asked for a battle design proposal. It was filed in
  `wiki/gameplay/battle.md § Battle rules` as [proposed] (Q-039).
