## Summary of what was decided

- D-0006 is accepted: species are immutable and content-addressed; owned
  Peerlings are instances referencing their species by CID.
- Progression in v1: a simple XP curve; no evolution; Peerlings never learn
  or change moves (resolves Q-010). The concrete XP numbers were drafted as a
  proposal.
- The LLM drafted a 12-type effectiveness chart for review (Q-008 stays open
  until approved).
- The LLM drafted the encounter-seed / beacon design for review (Q-029 stays
  open until approved).
