# 2026-10-06 — Exact rules for the v1 fun features approved

Kind: design conversation. The LLM asked for approval of the exact rules
(Q-055) and raised two choices: whether the Peerling of the Day should last
an in-game day (2 hours) or a full 24-hour day, and whether creators may earn
the "First found in the wild by …" credit for their own species (the
proposal excluded them).

## Designer's answer (verbatim)

> Approved, yeah extend to full 24h day and exclude creators from earning first-finder credit

## Summary of what was decided

- Q-055 approved: following Peerling, guardians, Peerling of the Day, first
  wild finder and biome index rules as proposed.
- Change: the Peerling of the Day lasts a full 24-hour day (288 epochs,
  starting at 00:00 UTC), not a 2-hour in-game day.
- Confirmed: a species' creator can't earn its first-finder credit.
