# 2026-10-06 — Peerdex, menus, audio and rest points

Kind: design conversation. The LLM proposed: (1) a Peerdex listing only
species the player has met (seen as silhouettes, caught as full cards),
counters, filters, shimmer badges, a "My creations" tab; (2) title screen,
pause menu, HUD, key bindings and settings, English only in v1; (3) hand-made
or licensed music and sound effects shipped with the app, and Peerling cries:
(a) none, (b) synthesized in the browser from the species CID (recommended),
(c) AI-generated per species; (4) rest points: interact to heal with effects
and a message, set the respawn point, save a snapshot, remind about backups,
show area info; optional fast travel between discovered rest points.

## Designer's answers (verbatim)

> 1. approved
> 2. approved
> 3. approved with b
> 4. approved but no fast travel

## Summary of what was decided

- Peerdex, menus/HUD/controls/settings and rest-point behaviour as proposed.
- Music and sound effects: hand-made or licensed, shipped with the app.
- Peerling cries are synthesized in the browser from the species CID (b).
- No fast travel.
