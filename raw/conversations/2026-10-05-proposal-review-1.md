# 2026-10-05 — Review of unapproved proposals, part 1

Kind: design conversation. The LLM grouped all 198 `[proposed]` markers into
Part 1 (technical details that follow from earlier decisions) and Part 2 (20
numbered design choices), and asked for approval.

## Designer's answer (verbatim)

> Part 1 - Approved
> Part 2 - 1,2,3,4 - Approved, but 5 we should look at. If the players are allowed to create new Peerlings with the same name as existing ones it will probably be confusing (and we end up with 50 variants all named "Pickachu") Can we easily require the Peerling name to be unique?

## Summary of what was decided

- Part 1 approved: all technical proposals (creation pipeline mechanics, data
  formats and CIDs, browser IPFS node, registry, saves and verification, PvP
  and trade mechanics, networking, determinism, resilience, trust rules).
- Part 2 items 1–4 approved: fixed house art style; example wishes and a
  300-character wish limit; concept contents (2–4 sentences of lore,
  personality, 3 name ideas); 30-second image cooldown.
- Item 5 (names 1–20 characters, not unique) is reopened: the designer wants
  species names to be unique to avoid confusion (e.g. 50 Peerlings called
  "Pikachu"), and asked whether that is easy (→ Q-041).
- Items 6–20 were not answered and stay proposed.
