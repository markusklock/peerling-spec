# 2026-10-06 — Extra v1 features: follower, guardians, Peerling of the Day, first finds

Kind: design conversation.

## Designer's request (verbatim)

> First I want you to do one more thing, give me a few suggestions for tech or features that would fit the game and make it more fun and enjoyable in its first version

## LLM suggestions (summary)

1. Your lead Peerling follows you: the first Peerling in your team walks behind
   you on the map, hopping along at the height of its size class; other
   players see it too.
2. Landmark guardians (12 badges): each biome's landmark has a guardian you
   battle for that biome's badge; the guardian team is drawn each week from
   that biome's Peerlings using the shared epoch randomness; badge wins are
   verified by replay like catches; no server needed.
3. Daily Spotlight: each in-game day the shared randomness picks one species
   that appears more often across the whole world; the world feed announces
   it and its creator is notified; no server needed.
4. First discoverer credit: the first player with a verified catch of a new
   species is shown on its card ("Discovered by …"); the world feed announces
   it.
5. Ghost battles against an offline player's team (AI-controlled).
6. Hearts for species.
7. Photo mode and catch clips (WebCodecs), shared via IPFS.

LLM recommendation: 1, 2 and 4; 5 as backup.

## Designer's answer (verbatim)

> 1 and 2 approved
> 3 approved but we should also showcase the daily spotlight Peerling in game, like a pedistal near the spawn area with a 3D model of the Peerling on top that can be hovered to explain that this is the Peerling of the Day
> 4 approved but should be called "First found in the wild by..." to make it clear that this was the first player who found this Peerling in the wild.

## Summary of what was decided

- Approved for v1: following Peerling (1), landmark guardians with 12 badges
  (2), the daily spotlight, named **Peerling of the Day** (3), and the first
  wild finder credit, shown as **"First found in the wild by …"** (4).
- Peerling of the Day addition: a pedestal near the spawn area with the
  Peerling's 3D model on top; hovering it explains that this is the Peerling
  of the Day.
- Not chosen: 5, 6, 7.
- Exact rules filling in the approved ideas are LLM proposals.
