# Battle System

> How fights between Peerlings work. Status: stub.

[accepted] Players fight wild Peerlings (Pokémon-inspired).

[proposed] Starting assumptions to confirm: turn-based, one active Peerling per
side, the player can switch Peerlings, use an item, try to catch, or flee.

To be specified: turn order (Speed), damage formula (stats, move power,
[type effectiveness](../peerlings/types.md), randomness), status effects,
stat stages, fainting, experience and rewards ([Q-010](../open-questions.md#q-010)),
battle presentation and animation of static models
([Q-015](../open-questions.md#q-015)).

## Requirements

- **BTL-001** [accepted] The player MUST be able to battle wild Peerlings they encounter.
