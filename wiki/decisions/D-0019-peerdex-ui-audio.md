# D-0019: Peerdex, menus, audio and rest points

**Status:** accepted (2026-10-06)

## Decision
[accepted]
- A **Peerdex** that lists only species the player has met ([peerdex](../gameplay/peerdex.md)).
- **Menus, HUD, controls and settings** as in [ui](../gameplay/ui.md); English
  only in v1.
- **Music and sound effects** are hand-made or licensed and shipped with the
  app; **Peerling cries** are synthesized in the browser from the species CID
  ([audio](../world/audio.md)).
- **Rest points** heal, set the respawn point, save a snapshot and remind
  about backups ([exploration § Healing and rest points](../gameplay/exploration.md#healing-and-rest-points)).
- **No fast travel.**

## Alternatives considered
- Peerling cries generated by an AI audio model per species (more GPU work and
  another asset per species), or no cries at all.
- Fast travel between discovered rest points: rejected; players walk.
